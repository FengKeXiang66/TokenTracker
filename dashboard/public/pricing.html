Free local use

Token collection, all supported provider integrations, cost estimates, provider limits, local charts, local exports and desktop features remain free. A Pro subscription is not required. Basic community leaderboard participation remains available to free accounts.

TokenTracker Pro

Cloud combines usage across computers in a personal account with web access, hosted history and exports, and synchronization/device management for up to five registered devices. Proposed history limits are 90 days of hourly detail and 24 months of daily summaries. Only usage metrics are synchronized. Prompts, responses, code, project/session records and AI provider credentials are not uploaded.
